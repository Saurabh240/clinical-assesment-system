Eligibility - Must meet criteria to proceed with assessment for minor ailment

Has a valid Ontario health care number
Is not precluded from receiving minor ailment services based on the claim maximums listed in Table 1 in the EO notice
Presents with one of the minor ailments listed below:
Allergic rhinitis Candidal stomatitis (oral thrush) Conjunctivitis (bacterial, allergic or viral) Dermatitis(atopic, eczema, allergic or contact)
Dysmenorrhea Gastroesophageal reflux disease Hemorrhoids Herpes labialis (cold sores) Impetigo Insect bites and urticaria (hives)
Musculoskeletal sprains and strains Tick bites, post-exposure prophylaxis to prevent Lyme disease Urinary tract infections (uncomplicated)
Aphthous ulcers Diaper dermatitis Acne Pinworms Nausea and vomiting of pregnancy Vaginal Candidiasis
Patient qualifies for minor ailment and presents no red flags that would automatically be referred to another care provider
