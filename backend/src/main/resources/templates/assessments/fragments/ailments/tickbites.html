Minor Ailment Assessment & Treatment Options - Tick Bites
RATIONALE FOR PRESCRIBING
Patient presents with the following symptoms consistent with a tick bite. (Check all that apply)
Inflammation
Redness
Itch
Pain
Other:                                                                                                                             
Patient meets the following criteria for minor ailment prescribing  (Check all that apply)
No erythema, migraines, bull's-eye" rash
No fever, chills, headache, stiff neck, fatigue, decreased appetite, muscle and joint aches, joint swelling, swollen lymph nodes
Must meet ALL 3 of the following criteria:
Tick has been removed within the past 72 hours. (If the tick is still attached, advise the patient to safely remove.
Tick bite occurred in an area where blacklegged ticks have been identified
Tick was likely attached for 36 hours or more
Prescription Treatment
Doxycycline:
Adults: 200 mg PO x 1 dose
Children: doxycycline 4 mg/kg (maximum dose of 200 mg) PO x 1 dose (no age restriction)
