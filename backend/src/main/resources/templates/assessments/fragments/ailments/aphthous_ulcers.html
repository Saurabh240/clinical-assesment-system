Minor Ailment Assessment & Treatment Options - Aphthous Ulcers
RATIONALE FOR PRESCRIBING
Patient presents with the following symptoms consistent with oral ulcers (Check all that apply)
1 - 5 round or oval, small, white/yellow/grey colored ulcer(s) with raised margin and red halo
Duration <14 days
Ulcer(s) present on mucosal surfaces; inside cheeks or lips, side of tongue, soft palate (the posterior roof of mouth), or floor of mouth
Ulcer(s) are shallow and painful
History of recurrent episodes <6 times per year
Other:                                                                                                                             
Patient meets the following criteria for minor ailment prescribing (Must check all)
Not immunocompromised due to disease state, medication, radiation therapy, or immunodeficiency disorder (e.g. HIV, malignancies, Behcet's syndrome, Reiter's syndrome, inflammatory bowel disease, celiac disease, systemic lupus erythematosus, etc.)
No history of ulcers that last longer than 14 days, or cause scarring;
Current lesion has been present <14 days
First occurrence of ulcers before the age of 30
No recent contact with someone exhibiting the same symptoms
Patient has no recently started a new drug (e.g. NSAIDs, beta-blockers, methotrexate, ACEI, antiarrhythmic, opioids, etc.) that could indicate drug-induced ulcers
No systemic symptoms (e.g. fever, pharyngitis, joint pain)
No lesions present elsewhere on the body e.g. head, trunk, hard palate (anterior roof of mouth), genitals, throat, lips, eyes, hands, feet
No severe pain that is inhibiting eating, drinking or swallowing
No recurrences > 6 times per year
Diameter of ulcer <1 cm
<5 ulcers present
No clusters of many (10 to 100) small ulcers, 1-3mm in diameter, that may coalesce into 1 irregular shaped ulcer
Prescription Treatment
Triamcinolone Acetonide 0.1%: dab small amount (0.5cm) to ulcer(s) QHS and as required up to TID PC PRN x 7 days (Do not rub in)
OTC Treatment
Benzocaine: Apply to affected area in the mouth up to QID
Lidocaine Viscous 2%: Apply to affected area in the mouth up to QID
Carboxymethyl Cellulose: Use as often as needed, particularly PC
