Minor Ailment Assessment & Treatment Options - Candidal Stomatitis
RATIONALE FOR PRESCRIBING
Patient presents with the following symptoms consistent with oral thrush (Check all that apply)
Superficial, gray-to-white curd-like spots on lips, buccal mucosa, tongue or palate
Plaque removal reveals erythematous base and cause mild bleeding
Fuzzy feeling in the mouth and/or loss of taste
Other:                                                                                                                             
Patient meets the following criteria for minor ailment prescribing  (Check all that apply)
No recent broad spectrum antibiotic or used inhaled corticosteroids
No dentures or significant swelling in the area
Lesions present < 3 weeks
Lesions not ulcerous or vesicular
Prescription Treatment
Nystatin 100,000 units/mL Swish & swallow 400 000 – 600 000 units QID PO × 7–14 days
Nystatin 100,000 units/mL Swish & swallow 100 000 units QID PO × 7–14 days (infant < 1 year old)
