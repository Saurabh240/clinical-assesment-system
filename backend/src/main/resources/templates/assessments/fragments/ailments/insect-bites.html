Minor Ailment Assessment & Treatment Options - Insect Bites and Urticaria
RATIONALE FOR PRESCRIBING
Patient presents with the following symptoms consistent with Insect Bites (Non-Tick)  (Check all that apply)
Area of bites expected after exposure to insects
Inflammation
Redness
Itch
Pain
Hives
Other:                                                                                               
Patient meets the following criteria for minor ailment prescribing (Must check all)
No redness around the bite that has expanded over the course of a few days and / or resembles a bulls-eye
No signs of an anaphylactic reaction
No extensive skin symptoms such as hives on areas other than the bite site
No lesions worsening / present for longer than 7 days
No lesions appear infected
No swollen lymph nodes or flu-like symptoms
PRESCRIPTION TREATMENT
Desonide 0.05%: Apply daily-BID PRN (maximum 7 days)
Hydrocortisone 0.5% - 2.5%: Apply daily-BID PRN (maximum 7 days)
Bilastine:
Adults and children ≥12 y: 20 mg once daily PO
Children 4–11 y (body wt ≥16 kg): 10 mg once daily PO
Cetirizine:
Adults and children ≥6 y: 5–10 mg Q24H PO; maximum 20 mg/day
Children: 6–12 months: 2.5 mg Q24H PO
12–23 months: 2.5 mg Q24H PO; maximum 2.5 mg Q12H
2–5 y: 2.5 mg Q24H PO; maximum 5 mg/day in 1–2 doses
Rupatadine:
Adults and children ≥12 y: 10 mg Q24H PO
Children 2–11 y: oral solution
10–25 kg: 2.5 mg Q24H PO; maximum 2.5 mg/day
>25 kg: 5 mg Q24H PO; maximum 5 mg/day
Hydroxyzine:
Adults: 25 mg TID to QID PO; maximum 100 mg/day
Children (up to 40 kg): 50–80 mg (or 2 mg/kg) daily, divided TID to QID; if >40 kg, adult dosing
Children <6 years: 30–50 mg (or 2 mg/kg) daily, divided TID to QID
OTC TREATMENT
Benzocaine 7.5% - 20%: apply TID–QID PRN for itching (maximum 7 days)
Pramoxine 1%: apply QID PRN
Camphor or menthol-containing products: 0.13–10.5%: apply sparingly PRN
Calamine lotion 1–16%: apply liberally PRN
Ammonia/baking soda 3.5% ammonia: apply sparingly PRN
Lidocaine 0.5–9.6%: apply as needed up to 3 times daily. Use sprays no more than twice per hour
Zinc Oxide 10%–40%: Apply as needed PRN
Desloratadine:
Adults and children ≥12 y: 5 mg Q24H PO
Children 6–11 months: 1 mg Q24H PO
1–5 y: 1.25 mg Q24H PO
6–11 y: 2.5 mg Q24H PO
Fexofenadine:
Adults: 60 mg Q12H PO; maximum 180 mg/day (12-h formulation) or 120 mg/day (24-h formulation)
Children 2–11 y: 30 mg Q12H PO
Loratadine:
Adults and children >5 y: 10 mg Q24H PO
Children 2–5 y: 5 mg Q24H PO
Chlorpheniramine:
Adults: 4 mg Q4–6H PO; maximum 24 mg/day
Children: 0.35 mg/kg/day PO given in 4 divided doses
Diphenhydramine:
Adults: 25–50 mg Q6–8H PO; maximum 300 mg/day
Children: 5 mg/kg/day PO given in divided doses
Cyproheptadine:
Adults: Initially 4 mg Q8H PO; then 0.5 mg/kg/day, usually 12–16 mg/day; max 32 mg daily
Children 2–6 years: 2 mg Q8–12H; max 12 mg daily
Children 7–14 years: 0.25 mg/kg/day PO or 8 mg/m²/day in 2–3 divided doses OR 4 mg Q8–12H; max 16 mg daily
