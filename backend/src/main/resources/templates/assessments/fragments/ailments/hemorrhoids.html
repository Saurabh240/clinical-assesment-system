Minor Ailment Assessment & Treatment Options - Hemorrhoids
RATIONALE FOR PRESCRIBING
Patient presents with the following symptoms consistent with hemorrhoids (Check all that apply)
Constipation
Straining with defecation
Pruritus or irritation in perianal area
Minimal bright red bleeding in rectal area, following a bowel movement
Minor anal pain
Other:                                                                                                                             
Patient meets the following criteria for minor ailment prescribing (Must check all)
Age > 12 and age > 50 with no new onset of symptoms
No recent physical trauma
No dark red rectal bleeding, blood mixed with stool, black tarry stool, severe pain upon defecation, abdominal pain or tenderness, perianal swelling, unexplained weight loss.
No personal or family history of polyps, colorectal cancer or IBD
No previous therapies tried within past 7 days
Prescription Treatment
Proctosedyl 1% ointment          g . Apply BID and after each bowel movement x 7 days
Proctosedyl suppositories            Insert BID and after each bowel movement x 7 days
Anusol HC ointment 0.5%          g. Apply BID and after each bowel movement x 7 days
Anusol HC suppositories  Insert BID and after each bowel movement x 7 days
OTC Treatment

Anusol Plus suppositories            Insert BID and after each bowel movement x 7 days
Preparation H-PE gel            g Apply BID and after each bowel movement x 7 days
