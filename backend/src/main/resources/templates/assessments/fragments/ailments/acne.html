Minor Ailment Assessment & Treatment Options - Acne
RATIONALE FOR PRESCRIBING
Patient presents with the following symptoms consistent with acne (Check all that apply)
Comedonal acne (white & blackheads)
Mild inflammatory acne (papules, pustules)
Previous diagnosis of acne
Other:                                                                                                                             
Patient meets the following criteria for minor ailment prescribing (Must check all)
> 12 yo
Not suspected drug-induced acne
No family history of scarring acne
No alarm signs or symptoms (fever/arthralgia, hyperandrogenism eg. scalp hair loss, facial hair, irregular menstrual cycle)
No presence of nodules or cysts
Approximately < 20 comedones present
Approximately < 15 inflammatory papules present
Total lesion count < 30
No erythema, papules, pustules in the absence of comedones (other conditions must be ruled out)
No widespread inflammatory lesions (i.e. involving areas beyond the face)
No high levels of anxiety/low self-esteem
New onset age < 30
Prescription Treatment
Clindamycin 1% Sol: Apply to entire affected area BID x 8–12 weeks
Azelaic Acid 15% Gel (Finacea): Apply to entire affected area BID x 8–12 weeks
Adapalene 0.1% or 0.3% (Differin): Apply to entire affected area QHS x 8–12 weeks
Tazarotene 0.045% cream or gel (Arazlo): Apply to entire affected area QHS x 8–12 weeks
Tretinoin 0.01% or 0.025% or 0.05% or 0.1% Gel or Cream: Apply to entire affected area QHS x 8–12 weeks
Trifarotene 0.005% (Aklief): Apply to entire affected area QHS x 8–12 weeks
Dapsone 5% Gel (Aczone): Apply to entire affected area BID x 8–12 weeks
Benzoyl Peroxide 10% Gel or Lotion or Wash: Apply to entire affected area QHS or BID x 8–12 weeks

Combination products:
Adapalene 0.1% or 0.3% & Benzoyl Peroxide 2.5% Gel (TactuPump): Apply to entire affected area QHS x 8–12 weeks
Clindamycin 1% & Benzoyl Peroxide 3% or 5% Gel (BenzaClin/Clindoxyl): Apply to entire affected area QHS x 8–12 weeks
Erythromycin 3%/Benzoyl Peroxide 5% Gel (Benzamycin): Apply to entire affected area BID x 8–12 weeks
Tretinoin 0.025%/Clindamycin 1.2% Gel (Biacna): Apply to entire affected area QHS x 8–12 weeks
OTC Treatment
Benzoyl Peroxide 2.5–5% Gel or Lotion or Wash: Apply to entire affected area BID x 8–12 weeks
Glycolic acid 2–15% Gel or Lotion or Cream: Apply to entire affected area BID x 8–12 weeks
Salicylic Acid 0.5–2%: Apply to entire affected area BID x 8–12 weeks
