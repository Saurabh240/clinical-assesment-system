Minor Ailment Assessment & Treatment Options - Vaginal Candidiasis
RATIONALE FOR PRESCRIBING
Patient presents with the following symptoms consistent with Vaginal Candidiasis (Check all that apply)
Vulvar and/or vaginal pruritis and irritation
Thick, white, "curd-like" vaginal discharge
Perceived external (not urethral) dysuria (painful urination) and/or dyspareunia (painful intercourse)
Vulvar burning/pain/discomfort
Vulvar edema/erythema
Sporadic or infrequent (≤ 3 episodes in the last 365 days, including this episode)
Mild to Moderate symptoms
Other:                                                                                                                             
Patient meets the following criteria for minor ailment prescribing (Must check all)
Not a high-risk patient (e.g., immunocompromised or pregnant)
No fever, pelvic pain, coloured or foul-smelling discharge
No severe disease: extensive redness, severe edema, excoriation, fissure
No thin and whitened vulvar skin (i.e., Lichen sclerosus), purple, itchy flat bumps on skin; white lacy patches on genital mucosa (i.e., Lichen planus),
No symptoms of vaginal atrophy (e.g., spotting/bleeding, dryness, frequent urination, etc.)
No hypoestrogenic state (e.g., peri/menopause (without HRT), oophorectomy, primary ovarian insufficiency, ovarian insufficiency, severe lupus erythematosus)
No potential for STI or co-infection (e.g., unprotected sex, multiple partners, anonymous partners, etc.)
No foreign body present (e.g., tampon or condom)
No allergic reaction or contact dermatitis brought on by products (e.g., soaps, vaginal preparations)
Patient has previous diagnosis of vaginal candidiasis
Patient is over 12 years of age (post-puberty) and not symptomatic prior to first menses
Not an individual born without a vagina
No previous episode within the last 8 weeks
No treatment failure of current episode
Prescription Treatment
Terconazole 0.4% Cream: One applicatorful (5g) vaginally + apply cream externally QHS x 7 days
OTC Treatment
Fluconazole
Fluconazole 150 mg PO as a single dose
Clotrimazole:
200 mg tablet vaginally daily for 3 days
500 mg tablet vaginally as a single dose
1% cream 1 applicatorful vaginally daily for 7 days
2% cream 1 applicatorful vaginally daily for 3 days
10% cream 1 applicatorful vaginally as a single dose
Miconazole
100 mg ovule vaginally daily for 7 days
2% cream 1 applicatorful vaginally daily for 7 days
4% cream 1 applicatorful vaginally daily for 3 days
