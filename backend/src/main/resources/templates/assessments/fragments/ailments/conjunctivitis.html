Treatment - Bacterial Conjunctivitis
Gramicidin/polymyxin B Drops: initially 1–3 drops in affected eye(s) Q1H, gradually decreasing to 1–2 drops 4–6 times daily × 7 days
Erythromycin Ointment: 1.25 cm 2–6 times/day in affected eye(s) × 7 days
Fusidic acid (over 2 years old) 1%: 1 drop in affected eye(s) Q12H × 7 days
Polymyxin B/trimethoprim (over 2 months old): 0.1%/10,000 units/mL Initially 1–3 drops in affected eye(s) Q1H, gradually decreasing to 1–2 drops Q3H daily × 7 days
Tobramycin (Over 1 year old): 0.3%:
Drops: 1–2 drops in affected eye(s) Q4H × 7 days
Ointment: 1.25 cm in affected eye(s) BID–TID × 7 days

Treatment - Viral Conjunctivitis
Antazoline/naphazoline (adults) 0.51%/0.051%: 1–2 drops in affected eye(s) Q3–4h x 3–4 days
Pheniramine/naphazoline (adults) 0.3%/0.025%: 1–2 drops in affected eye(s) QID x 3–4 days
Tetrahydrozoline (adults) 0.05%: 1–2 drops in affected eye(s) BID–QID x 3–4 days
Naphazoline (adults) 0.1%: 1–2 drops in affected eye(s) QID x 3–4 days
