Minor Ailment Assessment & Treatment Options - Impetigo
RATIONALE FOR PRESCRIBING
Patient presents with the following symptoms consistent with non-bullous impetigo (Check all that apply)
Small vesicles (blisters) surrounded by erythema
Lesions that burst, leaving a yellow-honey coloured, adherent crust with erosions and erythema surrounding the lesions
Tender, Itchy
Multiple vesicles but localized to central face or arms and legs
Other:                                                                                                                             
Patient meets the following criteria for minor ailment prescribing (Must check all)
Non-bullous blister/lesions
No systemic symptoms (e.g fever, malaise, chills)
Immunocompetent
No extensive skin lesions (< 3 lesion area)
Prescription Treatment
First Line
Fucidin Cream 15 g. Apply BID–TID x 7–14 days
Mupirocin Ointment 15 g. Apply BID–TID x 5 days
Second Line
Ozenoxacin 10g. Apply BID x 5 days
Bacitracin/gramicidin/polymyxin (Polysporin) 15g Apply TID for up to 7 days
Bacitracin 15g Apply 1–3 times daily for up to 7 days
