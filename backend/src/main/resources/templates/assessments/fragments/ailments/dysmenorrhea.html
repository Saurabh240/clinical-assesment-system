Minor Ailment Assessment & Treatment Options - Dysmenorrhea
RATIONALE FOR PRESCRIBING
Patient presents with the following symptoms consistent with dysmenorrhea (Check all that apply)
Lower mid-abdominal pain ± pelvic pain/cramping
Pain radiating to lower back and upper thigh
Pain begins with onset of menses
Pain lasts 2-3 days
Other:                                                           
Patient meets the following criteria for minor ailment prescribing (Must check all)
Initial onset of pain is within hours or onset of menstruation
No signs of systemic infections (e.g. fever, chills)
No IUD inserted in the last 1 year
No pain at other times of menstrual cycle
Age > 12 years old
No history of endometriosis, ovarian cysts, fibroids, cardiovascular or GI disease/disorder, renal impairment or bleeding disorder
Prescription Treatment
Ibuprofen 400 mg, loading dose of 800mg PO, then 400 mg PO Q6H x 3 days. Refills x 3
Naproxen 500 mg BID x 3 days. Refills x 3
Naproxen sodium 550 mg PO BID x 3 days. Refills x 3
Diclofenac potassium 50mg, loading dose 100 mg PO, then 50 mg Q6-8H PRN x 3 days. Refills x 3
Celecoxib 200 mg QD x 3 days. Refill x 3
Mefenamic 250mg, loading dose of 500mg PO then 250 mg PO Q6H x 3 days. Refills x 3
Ketoprofen 50 mg PO TID x 3 days. Refills x 3
Flurbiprofen 50 mg PO QID x 3 days. Refills x 3
