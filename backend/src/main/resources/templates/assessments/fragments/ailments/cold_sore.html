Minor Ailment Assessment & Treatment Options - Cold Sores
RATIONALE FOR PRESCRIBING
Patient present with the following symptoms of cold sores (Check all that apply)
Prodromal symptoms (itching, tingling, burning sensation) within 48 hours → prescribe antiviral
If any of the following are present, suggest OTC treatment
Lesions present < 14 days
Single painful lesion with multiple vesicles on the vermilion border of the lip and around the nostrils
Other:                                                                                                                             
Patient meets the following criteria for minor ailment prescribing  (Check all that apply)
Had at least 1 previous diagnosed cold sore
No lesions present on or around the nose or ocular area
No systemic illness (e.g fever)
Prescription Treatment
Valacyclovir 2000 mg BID PO x 1 day
Acyclovir 400mg 5 times daily PO x 5 days
Famciclovir 750 mg BID PO x 1 day
Famciclovir 1500 mg PO x 1 dose
Acyclovir (Zovirax Cream or Ointment) Start within 1 hr of onset of symptoms. Apply 5 times daily x 4 days
Acyclovir 5%/hydrocortisone 1% (Xerese) Adults and children >12 y: apply 5 times daily during waking hours x 5d starting immediately after onset of signs or symptoms
OTC Treatment Docosanol (Abreva) Apply to affected area at the onset of symptoms every 3 hours x 5–10 days
