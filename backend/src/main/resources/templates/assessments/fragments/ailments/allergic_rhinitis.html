Minor Ailment Assessment & Treatment Options - Allergic Rhinitis
RATIONALE FOR PRESCRIBING
Patient presents with the following symptoms consistent with allergic rhinitis (Check all that apply)
Congestion
Sneezing
Postnasal drip
Coughing
Runny nose
Headache
Sore throat
Hives
Itchy eyes, ears, mouth, throat, nose and/or skin
Allergic shiner (i.e. darkened or swollen areas under the eyes)
Other:                                                                                                                             
Patient meets the following criteria for minor ailment prescribing  (Check all that apply)
> 2 years of age
No concomitant uncontrolled asthma, otitis media, or sinusitis
Symptoms < 2 weeks with pharmacological treatment
No shortness of breath or difficulty breathing
Bilateral symptoms
No fever
No severe headache or eye pain
No close contact with an individual with an infectious disease or loss of smell/taste
No recurrent nosebleeds
PRESCRIPTION TREATMENT
Bilastine:
Adults and children ≥12 y: 20 mg once daily PO
Children 4–11 y (body wt ≥16 kg): 10 mg once daily PO
Cetirizine:
Adults and children ≥6 y: 5–10 mg Q24H PO; maximum 20 mg/day
Children: 6–12 months: 2.5 mg Q24H PO
12–23 months: 2.5 mg Q24H PO; maximum 2.5 mg Q12H
2–5 y: 2.5 mg Q24H PO; maximum 5 mg/day in 1–2 doses
Desloratadine:
Adults and children ≥12 y: 5 mg Q24H PO
Children 6–11 months: 1 mg Q24H PO
1–5 y: 1.25 mg Q24H PO
6–11 y: 2.5 mg Q24H PO
Fexofenadine:
Adults: 60 mg Q12H PO; maximum 180 mg/day (12-h formulation) or 120 mg/day (24-h formulation)
Children 2–11 y: 30 mg Q12H PO
Rupatadine:
Adults and children ≥12 y: 10 mg Q24H PO
Children 2–11 y: oral solution
10–25 kg: 2.5 mg Q24H PO; maximum 2.5 mg/day
>25 kg: 5 mg Q24H PO; maximum 5 mg/day
Loratadine:
Adults and children >5 y: 10 mg Q24H PO
Children 2–5 y: 5 mg Q24H PO
Beclomethasone:
Adults and children ≥6 y: 2 sprays (50 mcg/spray) in each nostril BID Adults: maximum 12 sprays/day. Children: maximum 8 sprays/day
Budesonide
Nasal suspension (64 mcg/metered dose):
Adults and children ≥6 y: Initial dose: 2 sprays in each nostril daily or 1 spray in each nostril BID; may decrease maintenance dose to 1 spray in each nostril daily
Nasal powder (100 mcg/dose):
Adults and children ≥6 y: Initial dose: 2 applications in each nostril in the morning or 1 application in each nostril BID; may decrease maintenance dose to 1 application in each nostril daily Max:400mcg/d
Ciclesonide:
Adults and children ≥12 y: 2 sprays (50 mcg/spray) in each nostril daily; maximum 200 mcg/day
Fluticasone propionate:
Adults and children ≥12 y: 2 sprays (50 mcg/spray) in each nostril daily, may increase to BID in severe situations; maximum 400 mcg/day
Children 4–11 y: 1–2 sprays in each nostril daily; maximum 200 mcg/day
Fluticasone furoate:
Adults and children ≥12 y: 2 sprays (27.5 mcg/spray) in each nostril once daily; maximum 110 mcg/day
Children 2–11 y: 1 spray in each nostril once daily, may increase to 2 sprays in each nostril once daily if needed. Decrease to 1 spray in each nostril daily for maintenance; maximum 110 mcg/day
Mometasone:
Adults and children ≥12 y: 2 sprays (50 mcg/spray) in each nostril daily, may decrease to 1 spray in each nostril daily for maintenance; may increase to BID in severe situations
Children 3–11 y: 1 spray in each nostril daily
Triamcinolone
Adults and children ≥12 y: 2 sprays (55 mcg/spray) in each nostril once daily, may decrease to 1 spray in each nostril daily
Children 4–11 y: 1 spray in each nostril once daily, may increase to 2 sprays in each nostril once daily if needed. Decrease to 1 spray in each nostril daily for maintenance; maximum 110 mcg/day
Azelastine/fluticasone:
Adults and children ≥12 y: 1 spray (137 mcg/50 mcg) in each nostril BID
OTC Treatment
Chlorpheniramine:
Adults: 4 mg Q4–6H PO; maximum 24 mg/day
Children: 0.35 mg/kg/day divided Q4–6H PO
Diphenhydramine:
Adults: 25–50 mg Q6–8H PO; maximum 300 mg/day
Children: 5 mg/kg/day PO given in 3–4 divided doses
Cyproheptadine:
Adults: Initially 4 mg Q8H PO; then 0.5 mg/kg/day, usually 12–16 mg/day; max 32 mg daily.
Children 2–6 years: 0.25 mg/kg/day PO OR 2 mg Q8–12H; max 12mg/d
Children 7–14 years: 0.25 mg/kg/day PO OR 4 mg Q8–12H; max 16mg/d
Pseudoephedrine:
Adults: 60 mg Q4–6H PO; maximum 240 mg/day
Sustained-release: 120 mg Q12H PO; maximum 240 mg/day
Children 6–11 y: 30 mg Q4–6H PO; maximum 120 mg/day
Cetirizine/pseudoephedrine:
Adults and children ≥12 y: 1 tablet (5 mg/120 mg) Q12H PO
Desloratadine/pseudoephedrine:
Adults and children ≥12 y: 1 tablet (2.5 mg/120 mg) Q12H PO
Fexofenadine/pseudoephedrine:
Adults and children ≥12 y: 1 tablet (60 mg/120 mg) Q12H PO
Loratadine/pseudoephedrine:
Adults and children ≥12 y: 1 tablet (5 mg/120 mg) Q12H PO or 1 tablet (10 mg/240 mg) Q24H PO
Oxymetazoline:
Adults and children ≥6 y: 0.05% solution: 2–3 drops/sprays per nostril (max 3 days)
Pheniramine/phenylephrine:
Adults and children ≥6 y: 0.25% or 0.5% solution: 2–3 drops/sprays per nostril Q4H
Xylometazoline:
Adults and children ≥6 y: 0.05% or 0.1% solution: 2–3 drops or 1–2 sprays per nostril Q8–10H
