Minor Ailment Assessment & Treatment Options - Musculoskeletal Sprains and Strains
RATIONALE FOR PRESCRIBING
Patient presents with the following symptoms consistent with musculoskeletal sprains and strains (Check all that apply)
Pain (mild to moderate)
Swelling
Tenderness
Bruising
Other:                                                                                                                             
Patient meets the following criteria for minor ailment prescribing  (Check all that apply)
No eye or head injury, nosebleeds or ruptured eardrum
No severe pain, obvious fractures or joint deformity
Able to bear weight on injured limb
Prescription Treatment
Acetaminophen 325–500 mg Q3H PRN PO OR 325–650 mg Q4H PRN PO OR 650–1000 mg Q6H PRN PO; Max 4 g/d
ASA 325–500 mg Q3H PRN PO OR 325–650 mg Q4H PRN PO OR 650–1000 mg Q6H PRN PO
Ibuprofen > 12 years old 600–800 mg TID–QID PRN
Naproxen 550 mg loading dose, then 275 mg Q6–8H PRN
Diclofenac diethylamine (Voltaren) Apply TID–QID x 7d
Diclofenac sodium (Pennsaid) Apply 2–4g TID–QID x 7d
Celecoxib 400mg single dose on the first day, then 100–200 mg daily PO PRN x 7d
Flurbiprofen 50mg Q4–6H PO PRN x 7d
Ketoprofen 25 or 50mg TID–QID PO PRN x 7d
Mefenamic acid 500mg loading dose, then 250 mg Q6H PO PRN x 7d
OTC Treatment
Ibuprofen
Adult: 200–400 mg Q4–6H PRN x 5 days
Children <12: 5–10 mg/kg/dose Q6–8H x 5 days
Naproxen sodium 220 mg Q8–12H PRN
