Minor Ailment Assessment & Treatment Options - GERD
RATIONALE FOR PRESCRIBING
Patient presents with the following symptoms consistent with GERD (Check all that apply)
Burning sensation back of throat
Worse when lying down, bending or after a meal
Regurgitation of acid or bile
Other:                                                                                                                             
Patient has one of the following symptom category
Mild (low intensity, no nocturnal symptoms, no affect on daily activities)
Infrequent (< 2 times per week)
Frequent (≥ 2 times per week)
Moderate (intense symptoms a few times a week, ± nocturnal symptoms)
Patient meets the following criteria for minor ailment prescribing  (Check all that apply)
No red flags present (abdominal mass, chest pain, choking, dysphagia, pain when swallowing, unintentional weight loss, vomiting, black tarry stool)
Age >18 years or age > 50 with no new onset of symptoms or worsening severity or frequency
No GI cancer in 1 degree relative
No recurrence ≤ 90 days of discontinuing successful acid suppression
TREATMENT
Mild and Infrequent
First Line
OTC H2RA ± Antacid or Alginate PRN x 2 weeks
Indicate the recommended OTC product:                                                                                                                             
Second Line
Cimetidine 300mg QID x 2 weeks
Famotidine 20mg BID x 2 weeks
Nizatidine 150mg BID x 2 weeks
Ranitidine 150mg BID x 2 weeks
Third Line
Dexlansoprazole 30 - 60 mg PO daily x 4 weeks
Esomeprazole 20 - 40 mg PO daily x 4 weeks
Lansoprazole 15 - 30 mg PO daily x 4 weeks
Omeprazole 20 - 40 mg PO daily x 4 weeks
Pantoprazole sodium 20 - 40 mg PO daily x 4 weeks
Pantoprazole magnesium 40 mg PO daily x 4 weeks
Rabeprazole 20 mg PO daily x 4 weeks
Moderate and Frequent
Dexlansoprazole 30 - 60 mg PO daily x 4 weeks + 1 refill
Esomeprazole 20 - 40 mg PO daily x 4 weeks + 1 refill
Lansoprazole 15 - 30 mg PO daily x 4 weeks + 1 refill
Omeprazole 20 - 40 mg PO daily x 4 weeks + 1 refill
Pantoprazole sodium 20 - 40 mg PO daily x 4 weeks + 1 refill
Pantoprazole magnesium 40 mg PO daily x 4 weeks + 1 refill
Rabeprazole 20 mg PO daily x 4 weeks + 1 refill
