Minor Ailment Assessment & Treatment Options - Diaper Dermatitis
RATIONALE FOR PRESCRIBING
Patient presents with the following symptoms consistent with dermatitis (Check all that apply)
Rash in creases / folds for > 3 days
Bright red or "beefy" red patches with a well demarcated scaly border or maceration of skin
Satellite papules and pustules
Present in areas bound by the specific boundaries of the diaper: around the lower abdomen and the lumbar back at the level of the umbilicus and below, around the upper thighs, encompassing the genitalia, perineum and buttocks.
Painful: crying when changing, during urination, or defecation
Patient meets the following criteria for minor ailment prescribing  (Must check all)
No acute onset with oozing, pus, vesicles or ulceration
No moderate or severe presentation (e.g. complicated, bleeding, skin erosion, etc.)
No systemic signs or symptoms (e.g. fever, nausea, vomiting)
Patient is not immunocompromised
No signs of abuse or neglect
No frequent recurrences especially with no intermittent rash-free period
No behavior changes (e.g. significant sleep disruption, crying, lethargy, etc.)
No complicated secondary infection, infection of genetalia, or co-morbid UTI
No rash present outside of the diaper and immediate surrounding area
Treatment - Diaper Dermatitis
OTC antifungal
Clotrimazole 1% cream: apply to affected area and surrounding skin BID x 14 days minimum
Miconazole 2% cream: apply to affected area and surrounding skin BID x 14 days minimum
Nystatin 100,000 Units/g cream: apply to affected area and surrounding skin BID-TID x 14 days minimum
Ketoconazole 2% cream (Over 12 yo): OD - BID x 14 days
Ciclopirox olamine 1% apply to affected area and surrounding skin BID x 28 days minimum
Hydrocortisone acetate:
0.5%          g apply a thin layer to the affected area up to TID for no more than 2 weeks
1%          g apply a thin layer to the affected area up to TID for no more than 2 weeks (Rx <2 yo)
