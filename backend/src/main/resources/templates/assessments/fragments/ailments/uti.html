Minor Ailment Assessment & Treatment Options – UTI
RATIONALE FOR PRESCRIBING
Patient presents with the following symptoms consistent with UTI (Check all that apply)
Dysuria
Frequency/Urgency
Suprapubic discomfort
No vaginal discharge
Patient meets the following criteria for minor ailment prescribing (Must check all)
Previous diagnosis of UTI
Age > 12, not pregnant or breastfeeding
No previous UTI within a month
No red flags present (abnormal urinary tract function/structure, symptoms lasting > 2 weeks, renal impairment, poorly controlled diabetes, immunocompromised)
No red flag symptoms present (fever, chills, malaise, flank or back pain, significant hematuria)
Not male
Prescription Treatment
First Line
Nitrofurantoin (MacroBID) 100mg PO BID x 5 days
Second Line
Sulfamethoxazole/trimethoprim (SMX/TMP) 800/160mg BID x 3 days
Trimethoprim 100mg BID x 3 days
Fosfomycin 3g dissolved in 1/2 cup of cold water OD x 1 day
